Dubai Smart Property Explorer

CS333 · Part 3: Interactive Visualization · Dubai sales of units and villas, January 2015 to July 2026

Dashboard Filters
  • Dropdowns: choose the property type and the registration type. Every chart updates.
  • Map: click a colored community to filter the other charts; double-click to reset. Grey can mean fewer than 20 eligible sales, no data, or unmatched community codes.
  • Scatter: drag horizontally to select a size range (m²). The map, timeline, box plot and heatmap update. Double-click to reset.
  • Timeline: compares quarterly median prices for Existing and Off-Plan properties. Drag to select a period; double-click to reset.
  • Hover over any mark for details. Drag updates are rate-limited for smoother interaction; calculations still use all eligible sales. Prices are per m² sale prices, not investment returns.
  • If a selection leaves no matching sales, double-click the interactive charts to reset their selections.
